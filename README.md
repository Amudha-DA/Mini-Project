# Power BI Business Intelligence Portfolio

## Project Overview
This repository contains a collection of Power BI projects designed to transform raw data into actionable business insights. The projects demonstrate proficiency in data cleaning (Power Query), relational data modeling, DAX calculations, and AI-powered analytics.

---

## 1. E-Commerce Sales & Target Analysis
A multi-stage analysis of retail transactions and performance against set targets.

### Technical Highlights:
* **ETL Processes:** Imported and transformed "List of Orders" and "Sales Target" data. Handled data inconsistencies and performed column transformations in Power Query.
* **DAX & Modeling:** * Created **Calculated Columns** to merge categories and sub-categories into a single 'Category Type'.
    * Developed **Measures** to track Total Sales, Profit Margins, and performance against monthly targets.
* **Visualizations:** * **Funnel Charts:** To visualize order distribution across different states.
    * **Trend Analysis:** Comparing monthly actual sales against predefined targets using combination charts.

---

## 2. Fleet Performance & Delivery Efficiency
An advanced dashboard focused on logistics and operational Al-driven insights.

### Key Features:
* **Data Cleaning:** Implemented mean imputation to handle missing fuel consumption values.
* **AI Visuals & Root Cause Analysis:**
    * **Decomposition Tree:** Analyzed "Cost per KM" by breaking it down through Vehicle Type, Maintenance Cost, and Distance.
    * **Key Influencers:** Identified the primary factors (Distance, Vehicle Type, Driver ID) that impact delivery status.
    * **Q&A Integration:** Configured natural language queries for instant reporting (e.g., "Average cost per KM by vehicle type").
* **Logistics KPIs:** Tracking on-time delivery rates and maintenance efficiency across various vehicle segments (Trucks, Vans, Mini-Trucks).

---

## Technical Skills Demonstrated
* **Power Query:** Merging, appending, pivoting, and cleaning complex datasets.
* **Data Modeling:** Establishing relationships between Fact and Dimension tables (Star Schema).
* **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Writing complex logic for business metrics and time-intelligence.
* **AI & Advanced Analytics:** Leveraging built-in Power BI AI tools for automated insight generation.
